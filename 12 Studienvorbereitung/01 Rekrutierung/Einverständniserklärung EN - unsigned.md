### Declaration of consent for the study: 
# HRZ Login Page Design vs. Own Login Page Design with focus on the workflow

## Welcome to our study!
Thank you for participating in our study as part of a bachelor thesis. In this study, the current HRZ login website design is tested against a newly developed login website design in terms of workflow.

This document describes the conditions and the process of your participation.

## Purpose of this study
The aim of this study is to test suggestions for improving the HRZ login website design and to evaluate their potential for improvement and make them available to the HRZ.

## Your contribution to the study
During this study, you will fill out questionnaires in presence, test the HRZ login website and the customized login website within a prototype solving a set of tasks in our study room or in your office on our setup and provide feedback.

You will be asked to share your current thoughts (Think Aloud) while testing the login web pages to give us a better insight into your thought processes, decision making and any difficulties.

## Procedure
The following is a detailed description of the study procedure:

0. You will sign this consent form before you participate in the study and submit it to us either online before the study begins or in person at the study meeting. You have the option to withdraw your consent for the study during and after participation.
1. After a short introduction, you fill out a questionnaire that asks for general and demographic data.
2. You test one of our prototypes and provide feedback in the form of a questionnaire.
3. You test the other prototype and again give feedback on it in the form of a questionnaire.
4. Finally, there is a short personal debriefing.

The study is designed for a half hour maximum of one hour.

## Sponsorship
This study is conducted by a student of the Rheinische Friedrichs-Wilhelms-Universität Bonn under the supervision of researchers led by Dr. Christian Tiefenau.

## Risks
No foreseeable mental or physical risks are to be expected.

## Confidentiality and data protection
Sound and screen recordings will be made during the study to give us a more detailed impression of the interaction with the prototypes. **No** visual recordings of your person will be made.

The data collected and recorded in this study will be treated confidentially at all times in accordance with the GDPR and stored pseudo-anonymously during the study period. Afterwards, the data will be anonymized and you will receive a participant ID from us so that we can identify your data if you withdraw your participation and delete it on request.

Any analysis of the data will only be performed on the anonymous data and at no time will the results be linked to identifying information.

In all cases, the use of collected and recorded data is subject to the standard data use guidelines of the Rheinische-Friedrich-Wilhelms-Universität Bonn.

## Data analysis and publication
Researchers and conducting students will only have access to this data if they agree to the confidentiality of the data and agree to the conditions stated in this document. No identifying information will be used in the publication and presentation of the study.

## Participation
Your participation in this study is entirely voluntary and you may withdraw your participation both during and after the study by providing your participant ID. If you choose to withdraw, you may do so without fear of penalty and all your anonymized data associated with your participation will be deleted.

If you have any questions or concerns at any time during the study, or if you believe that your participation in the study will have a negative impact, please contact the researcher conducting the study or Dr. Christian Tiefenau.
