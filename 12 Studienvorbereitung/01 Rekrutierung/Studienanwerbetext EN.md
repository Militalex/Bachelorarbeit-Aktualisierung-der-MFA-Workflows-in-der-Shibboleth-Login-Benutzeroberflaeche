## Context
As you may have already noticed, the University IT and Data Center (German: Hochschulrechenzentrum, HRZ) is planning to introduce mandatory two-factor authentication for all employees using so-called YubiKeys. These physical security keys, which are available in the form of USB sticks (aka. YubiKeys), will be used as a second factor after logging in with a Uni-ID and password.

Currently, the rollout of these YubiKeys is running rather slowly and the HRZ is facing many problems in this regard. In addition, the Shibboleth login page of the University of Bonn is currently not user-friendly enough for a wider range of users.

## Study
In order to support the HRZ with the problem described above, its login page design is to be adapted in the course of a bachelor thesis with a focus on the registration workflow in order to enable a more familiar, user-friendly experience.

In a usability study lasting a maximum of one hour, two prototype websites will be tested in presence here at the institute (department 4) in our study room (room number to be announced): the current login page design of the HRZ vs. our own developed page design.

The aim is to evaluate the improvement potential of the individual suggestions for improvement and to make them available to the HRZ.
